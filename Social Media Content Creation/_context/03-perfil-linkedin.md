## 2. Decisión de idioma del perfil

El **contenido** es ES-primario (comunidad semilla). Pero el **perfil** sirve a la audiencia más amplia y estratégica — investors, builders globales, talento — que es mayormente EN, y la comunidad ES conecta vía los posts, no la bio.

**Decisión (2026-06-03):** perfil **EN-primary** — headline + About en inglés. La línea ES de cierre quedó como **opcional** (no incluida en la versión aprobada del About · disponible si se quiere abrir más explícitamente la puerta al operador). La comunidad ES conecta vía los posts, no la bio.

## 4. About / Acerca de

> **✅ Versión aprobada (2026-06-03).** Construida sobre el About que Alan ya tenía + el frame laboratorio + el reframe del antagonista + el ángulo cliente/producto (su diferenciador: ingeniero que también piensa mercado/cliente). Calibrada contra `02-voz-y-estilo`.

```
I build systems for real-world operations.

That thinking was shaped across industries and countries — precision engineering in
Germany, software delivery across Latin America, enterprise data at Teradata. Each
environment added a layer. The foundation stayed the same: structure before
execution, architecture before automation.

For the last few years I've run my own lab — building things, breaking them,
learning what survives real conditions and what doesn't.

Today I apply that same thinking to AI. Not as hype, but as operational intelligence
infrastructure. Anyone can build a product or a technical solution — but what about
the client's perspective? The right market segment? The real customer pains? The
product strategy underneath? Building something that stays stable under real
operational and client pressure — that's the real challenge.

Right now I'm building at the intersection of applied AI and operational complexity:
environments where the problem was never technology. Restaurants, manufacturing
floors, distribution, logistics — places where the system that holds everything
together lives in someone's head, and breaks the moment they can't be everywhere at
once.

I've spent the last few years studying this up close, with my own eyes — talking to
the people who live it, watching where the real friction is.

I write about what I learn in the process: the technical and architectural
decisions, the reflections, and the whole journey.

If you're building in complex environments, or you live the operational reality of
one of them, I'm interested in that conversation. I care more about conversations
than followers.
```

**Notas:**
- Conserva el **reframe del antagonista** (*"lives in someone's head, and breaks the moment they can't be everywhere at once"*, NO *"absence of a system"*).
- **Ángulo cliente/producto** (segmento · customer pains · estrategia) = diferenciador: ingeniero que también piensa negocio.
- **Stealth:** restaurantes aparece como uno de varios dominios (no como EL foco) · no nombra el producto.
- **Opcionales no incluidos** (disponibles si los quieres): la línea ES de cierre para el operador · la firma *"Not the narrative — the real experience"*.
