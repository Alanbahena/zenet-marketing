**Where Zenet trails (acknowledged gaps for roadmap):**

- 🚧 KDS — roadmap (vs PoloTab, Parrot, Toteat, Fudo, Last.app, SoftRestaurant ship)
- 🚧 Voice stock counting — roadmap (only Apicbase ships)
- 🚧 MCP integration ChatGPT/Claude — roadmap (only Apicbase ships, emerging design pattern)
