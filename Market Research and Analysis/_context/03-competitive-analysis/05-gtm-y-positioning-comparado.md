### 5.3 Pattern recognition — what works, what doesn't

**Effective patterns (resonant):**

1. **Mexican identity explicit** (Parrot *"#1 en México"*, Wansoft *"100% tecnología mexicana"*, SoftRestaurant *"#1 de México"*) — Mexican operators respond
2. **Quantified savings/metrics** (MarginEdge 4% forecast accuracy, Loaded 8% margin growth, 29 days saved) — operator-actionable
3. **Operator credibility** (Loaded Cook Brothers Bars founders, MarginEdge "founded by former operators") — trust accelerator
4. **AI-native framing** (Apicbase, Nory, Restoke) — emerging differentiator
5. **Anti-rental simplicity** (SICAR "sin rentas mensuales") — Mexican price-sensitivity acknowledgment

**Less effective patterns:**

1. Generic functionality framing ("software para restaurantes") — undifferentiated
2. Tenure-only narrative ("desde 1980") — sounds legacy, NOT advantage
3. Enterprise scale claims ("locales más exitosos del mundo") — alienates SMB
4. AI marketing claims sin evidence shipped (Parrot 3,200 POS dataset BI as "AI") — risk credibility

**Anti-messaging:** Avoid generic "software para restaurantes" framing. Avoid tenure claims (Zenet pre-PMF). Avoid enterprise scale alienation. Avoid AI marketing claims without evidence (Zenet must ship AI Day 1 substantively).
