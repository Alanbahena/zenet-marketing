## 2. Methodology canonical · 3 sources triangulation

### 2.1 Las 3 methodologies

| Source | Tipo signal | Weight Phase 1 |
|---|---|---|
| **Van Westendorp PSM** | Quantitative directional · 4 puntos pricing | **15%** (N=3-5 statistical weak · directional only) |
| **Qualitative WTP interviews** | Multi-stakeholder context · objection mapping · willingness sustained | **35%** (small N deep insight) |
| **Behavioral data observation** | Revealed preference · actual usage · upgrade triggers · payment behavior | **50%** (revealed > stated) |

### 2.2 Triangulación weighting Phase 1

```
Behavioral (50%) — revealed preference dominates
   ↑
Qualitative (35%) — context informs
   ↑
PSM (15%) — directional anchor only
```

**Razón weighting:** pre-PMF N=3-5 design partners reality requires disciplinaweighting toward revealed > stated.

### 2.3 Disciplina anti-overinterpretation

**PSM con N=3-5 NO statistical significance · solo directional output.**

- ❌ NO tratar PSM OPP como "el precio óptimo"
- ❌ NO usar PSM results en investor pitch como "validated pricing"
- ❌ NO collapse 3 scenarios pricing basado solo en PSM output
- ✅ Use PSM como sanity check · range identification · outlier detection
- ✅ Always cross-reference con qualitative + behavioral signals
- ✅ Wait for N>10 (Phase 1.5+ post commercial launch) para PSM weight increase

## 3. Van Westendorp PSM methodology detail

### 3.1 Las 4 preguntas canónicas

Cliente recibe en este orden específico (anti-anchoring sequence):

| # | Pregunta | Captura |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"¿A qué precio considerarías Zenet TAN CARO que NO lo comprarías?"* | "Too expensive" ceiling |
| 2 | *"¿A qué precio considerarías Zenet TAN BARATO que dudarías de su calidad?"* | "Too cheap" floor (quality signal) |
| 3 | *"¿A qué precio empezarías a pensarlo bien — caro pero no descartado?"* | "Expensive but acceptable" |
| 4 | *"¿A qué precio consideras Zenet una ganga — gran compra por el dinero?"* | "Cheap but acceptable" |

**Anti-pattern crítico:** NUNCA preguntar "¿cuánto pagarías?" directo · destroys signal por adverse selection.

### 3.2 4 intersection points · cómo interpretar

Cuando ploteas 4 respuestas (5 design partners), las curvas se cruzan en 4 puntos:

| Punto | Cómo se forma | Qué representa |
|---|---|---|
| **PMC** (Point of Marginal Cheapness) | "Too cheap" × "Expensive but acceptable" | Floor del acceptable range · debajo cliente cuestiona calidad |
| **PME** (Point of Marginal Expensiveness) | "Too expensive" × "Cheap but acceptable" | Ceiling del acceptable range · arriba cliente se va |
| **OPP** (Optimal Price Point) ⭐ | "Too cheap" × "Too expensive" | Sweet spot · price con menor resistance |
| **IPP** (Indifference Price Point) | "Expensive but acceptable" × "Cheap but acceptable" | Precio "value/price" indifferent · típicamente cerca de median willingness |

### 3.3 Willingness ladder Phase 1 hipótesis

`[Hipótesis sin validar]` · 5 puntos Esencial baseline:

| Punto | MXN/sucursal/mes |
|---|---|
| 1 | $1,000 (downside floor signal) |
| 2 | $1,300 (downside-anchor bridge) |
| 3 | **$1,500 (anchor scenario)** ⭐ |
| 4 | $1,800 (anchor-upside bridge) |
| 5 | $2,100 (upside ceiling signal) |

**Refinement trigger:** post-pilot con primer design partner Mes 3 · adjust ladder si responses cluster fuera del range.

### 3.4 Tier-specific PSM Phase 1

| Tier | PSM applicable? | Detalle |
|---|---|---|
| **Esencial** | ✓ Sí | Baseline ladder $1,000-$2,100 |
| **Pro** | ✓ Sí | Adjusted ladder $1,400-$2,500 |
| **Multi-Sitio** | ✓ Sí (per-sucursal pricing) | Ladder $1,200-$2,200/sucursal · ICP arquetípico tier focus |
| **Enterprise** | ❌ NO | Consultative pricing · NO list price · PSM NO applicable |

### 3.5 PSM execution mecánica

`[Hipótesis sin validar · pilot validation post-first-design-partner]`:

| Aspect | Phase 1 hipótesis |
|---|---|
| Format | Conversational interview · NOT online survey impersonal |
| Duration | 15-20 min within longer 90-min Mes 3 GO/NO-GO interview |
| Researcher | Founder Alan (Phase 1) · CS Lead post-seed |
| Context priming | Cliente received valor 3 meses · informed responses · NO cold survey |
| Recording | Audio + notes · cliente consent per Adendum SF reciprocidad |
| Anti-anchoring discipline | NO mentioning current Zenet pricing antes de las 4 preguntas |

### 3.6 PSM output interpretation Phase 1

**Directional outputs only:**

- Acceptable range (PMC-PME)
- Sweet spot indication (OPP)
- Indifference point (IPP)
- Outliers identified (1+ partner fuera de cohort range)

**Lo que PSM output NO es:**

- ❌ NO "el precio óptimo final"
- ❌ NO "validated pricing decision"
- ❌ NO single-source decisional input

**Lo que PSM output SÍ es:**

- ✅ Directional range para triangulate con qualitative + behavioral
- ✅ Outlier identification (cohort sanity check)
- ✅ Anchor sanity check (current pricing within cohort acceptable range?)

## 4. Qualitative WTP interview methodology

### 4.1 Interview structure canonical · 60-90 min

| Section | Duration | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Warmup | 5 min | Rapport · context check · ¿cómo va la semana? |
| Value perception capture | 15 min | Qué value específico recibe · qué jobs done · qué pains resueltos |
| WTP qualitative exploration | 15 min | Open-ended willingness · multi-stakeholder consideration |
| Pricing scenario reactions | 10 min | React a current anchor · upside · downside (3 scenarios cf. doc 00 §6.4) |
| Competitor anchor reactions | 5 min | Reactions a Bistrosoft $1,599 · PoloTab · vertical AI premium global |
| PSM 4 questions (Mes 3 only) | 15-20 min | When applicable (Mes 3 GO/NO-GO checkpoint) |
| Wrap + next steps | 5 min | Feedback · what to do next |

### 4.2 Sample script Phase 1 · value perception capture

```
Researcher: "[Nombre], llevas 3 meses con Zenet operando. 
Quiero entender desde tu perspectiva qué es lo que más valor 
te ha generado · qué cosas que antes te dolían ya no te duelen."

[Cliente responde · researcher captures themes specific]

Researcher: "De todo lo que mencionas · ¿qué dirías que es 
lo que MÁS te ayuda · ese momento de 'esto vale lo que pago'?"

[Cliente identifies peak value moment · researcher notes]

Researcher: "Y si te quitara mañana esa parte específica · 
¿qué pasaría · cuánto te costaría reemplazarlo?"

[Forces willingness to pay context · captures substitute cost]
```

### 4.3 Multi-stakeholder considerations · separate interviews per role

Multi-threading 68% load-bearing canonical · cada role captures different signal:

| Role | Interview length | Focus questions |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** (paga · approval final) | 60-90 min | Financial decision · ROI perception · WTP total cliente · payment behavior signals |
| **Manager** (usa diariamente) | 30-45 min | Operational value · workflow impact · per-user perception · cap users adequacy |
| **Chef** (adoption critical) | 30-45 min | Adoption value · recetario standardization · indirect WTP signal via stickiness |
| **Contable externo** (silent veto) | 30 min | CFDI compliance · LFPDPPP comfort · authorization speed signal |

### 4.4 Reciprocidad framing (NO transactional)

Qualitative interviews son **Socio Fundador commitment per Adendum SF** (cf. doc 02 §7.4):

- ✅ NO bribery · NO compensation per interview
- ✅ Framed como reciprocidad programa (cliente received descuento 25% lock-in · provides feedback)
- ✅ Honest exit conversation expected si interview load excesivo

### 4.5 Anti-patterns en qualitative interviews

| NO hacer | Razón |
|---|---|
| Researcher reveals own pricing hypothesis | Anchor contamination · destroys signal independence |
| Interviewing solo Owner | Single-thread bias · multi-stakeholder 68% load-bearing lost |
| Sharing other partners' answers durante interview | Cohort contamination · destroys signal independence |
| Asking "¿cuánto pagarías?" sin context | Adverse selection · low signal · context-stripped |
| Treating interview answers as "lo que dijo es lo que es" | Stated vs revealed gap · always cross-check behavioral |

## 5. Behavioral data tracking · metrics canonical

### 5.1 Por qué behavioral > stated preference

**Revealed preference (actual behavior) supera stated preference (interview answers) en accuracy:**

- Stated: "Sí, pagaría más" → cliente NO upgrade cuando se ofrece
- Revealed: cliente eligió annual prepay con descuento → clear commitment signal
- Stated: "Es caro pero acceptable" → cliente cancela Mes 6 sin discount
- Revealed: cliente referido 2 peer operators → strong validation

**Disciplina canonical Phase 1:** behavioral weighted 50% · qualitative 35% · PSM 15%.

### 5.2 Behavioral metrics canonical Phase 1

| Metric | Captura | Frequency | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active users actual per sucursal | Cap users validation (doc 03 #2) | Mensual | Product analytics PostHog |
| Interactions/mes/sucursal | Internal caps + COGS validation + intensity profile (doc 04 §3.1) | Daily aggregation · monthly reports | Langfuse + custom dashboards |

## 6. Multi-stakeholder validation framework

### 6.1 Buying committee load-bearing canonical

`[Anclado en research]` (`02-customer-research/05-buying-process.md` v0.7 §10 · multi-threading 68% vs single-thread 23% conversion):

> **WTP NO se reduce a single number. Each stakeholder contributes distinct signal · all required para defensible pricing decision.**

### 6.2 Role-by-role WTP signal capture

| Role | Signal captured | Methodology source |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | WTP financial total · ROI perception · payment decision authority | Direct PSM + qualitative + payment behavior |
| **Manager** | Operational value perceived · workflow impact · per-user value | Qualitative + behavioral usage intensity |
| **Chef** | Adoption rate · recetario engagement · indirect WTP via stickiness | Behavioral primarily (adoption rate signal) |
| **Contable externo** | Authorization speed · CFDI compliance comfort · LFPDPPP confidence · silent veto risk | Qualitative interview + CFDI process behavior |

### 6.3 Reconciliation framework cuando roles diverge

**Owner pays → final decision authority** · BUT informed by:

| Signal divergence pattern | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Owner high WTP + Manager low usage | Owner enthusiasm vs actual adoption → adoption risk Mes 6 cliff · investigate Manager onboarding gap |
| Owner low WTP + Manager high usage | Manager extracting value but Owner NO seeing ROI in financials → ROI visibility gap · CS intervention |
| Owner high WTP + Contable veto | Silent veto risk · CFDI/LFPDPPP issues · Mes 6 cancellation risk · investigate Contable concerns immediately |
| Multi-Sitio Owner WTP vs Sucursal Manager WTP divergence | Cross-sucursal value perception inconsistency · Multi-Sitio tier validation question |

### 6.4 Anti-pattern · simplified WTP

❌ **NUNCA reduce multi-stakeholder analysis a single WTP number.**

❌ NO usar promedio de respuestas Owner+Manager+Chef+Contable.

✅ **SÍ preserve role-specific signals separately + reconcile via Owner final decision informed by all signals.**

## 8. Triangulation framework · synthesis 3 signals

### 8.1 3-signal synthesis canonical

```
PSM directional output (acceptable range + OPP indication)
   |
   | 15% weight
   ↓
   +
Qualitative interview themes (value perception · objection patterns · WTP sustained)
   |
   | 35% weight
   ↓
   +
Behavioral data evidence (payment behavior · upgrade triggers · churn signals · usage)
   |
   | 50% weight
   ↓
TRIANGULATED WTP estimate per tier
   ↓
v0.2 pricing decision (collapse 3 scenarios to anchor)
```

### 8.2 Conflict resolution matrix

Cuando 3 signals diverge:

| PSM signal | Qualitative signal | Behavioral signal | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low ($1,200 OPP) | Low ("muy caro") | Low (price-related churn) | **Adopt downside scenario** |
| Mid ($1,500-1,800) | Mid (acceptable) | Mid (sustained payment) | **Maintain anchor** |
| High ($1,800-2,100) | High ("vale lo que cobra") | High (annual prepay take-rate · zero price churn) | **Activate upside scenario** |
| PSM high · qualitative high · behavioral LOW | | | **Trust behavioral · revealed > stated · investigate churn patterns** |
| PSM high · qualitative LOW · behavioral high | | | **Trust behavioral · revealed preference dominates** |
| PSM LOW · qualitative high · behavioral high | | | **Trust behavioral + qualitative · PSM N=3-5 weak** |
| PSM LOW · qualitative LOW · behavioral high | | | **Surprising · investigate · likely product-market fit signal · revealed dominates** |

### 8.3 Weighting framework Phase 1

| Source | Weight | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioral | 50% | Revealed preference > stated · highest accuracy |
| Qualitative | 35% | Context · objection patterns · multi-stakeholder signal |
| PSM | 15% | Directional only · N=3-5 statistical weak |

**Phase 1.5+ post commercial launch (N>10):** PSM weight may increase to 25-30% with statistical significance.

### 8.4 Multi-stakeholder reconciliation overlay

Triangulation framework applied per role · then reconciled:

- Owner triangulated WTP = primary decision input (paga · approval authority)
- Manager triangulated signals = adoption risk indicator
- Chef triangulated signals = stickiness indicator
- Contable triangulated signals = silent veto risk indicator

**Owner final decision informed by all stakeholder triangulations · NOT averaged.**

## 10. Triggers framework · cuándo activar pricing changes

### 10.1 Trigger anchor maintain (most likely outcome)

**Adoptar si TODOS los criterios met:**

| Criterion | Threshold |
