## 2. Frame · outreach philosophy Phase 0-1

### 2.1 Core philosophy

> **Outreach Phase 0-1 es relationship-first NOT volume-first · peer reference > cold acquisition (MX cultural canonical) · founder-led trust building · discovery booking (NOT immediate sale) es el goal.**

### 2.2 4 principios canónicos

| Principio | Detalle |
|---|---|
| **Warm > cold siempre** | Network referrals + warm intros prioritized · cold selective only · MX SMB peer reference dominante (cf. `02-customer-research/06-objeciones.md` v0.5 §4.9) |
| **In-person primary Phase 0** | TJ geographic · founder physical presence trust building · in-person evaluation imperativo GTM #7 |
| **Discovery booking = success** | Outreach goal NO es vender · es agendar discovery interview · low-commitment ask |
| **Quality targeting > volume spray** | 10-20 targeted touches/sem > 100 cold spam · relationship-first MX cultural |

### 2.3 Anti-philosophy

| NO hacemos | Razón |
|---|---|
| Mass cold email/DM campaigns | Spray-and-pray · MX cultural misfit · brand damage |
| WhatsApp cold outreach pre-firma | Post-firma CS channel canonical · commoditiza moat |
| Premature pitch (skip discovery) | Discovery-first canonical · trust building precede selling |
| SDR/BDR hire Phase 0 | Founder visibility imperative #3 · founder-led canonical |

## 3. Outreach channels + priority hierarchy

### 3.1 Priority hierarchy canonical (5 tiers)

| Priority | Channel | Conversion expectation (discovery booked) | Volume Phase 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (highest)** | **Network referrals** (founder's TJ/BC network · existing operators known) | High (30-50%) | Primary |
| **2** | **Warm intros** (mutual connection introduces) | High (25-45%) | Secondary primary |
| **3** | **In-person events/visits** (CANIRAC · industry · direct visits) | Medium-High (20-35%) | Selective |
| **4** | **LinkedIn DM warm** (inbound signal → DM follow-up) | Medium (15-25%) | Reactive (post doc 02 signals) |
| **5 (lowest)** | **Cold outreach** (selective · NEVER spam) | Low (5-15%) | Minimal Phase 0 |

`[Hipótesis sin validar]` · conversion expectations refine post first 20 outreach attempts.

### 3.2 Channel detail per tier

**Tier 1 · Network referrals**
- Founder's existing relationships: operadores TJ/BC known · industry contacts · prior business relationships
- Highest trust (pre-established relationship) · highest conversion
- Socios Fundadores 1-3 referrals/año compound (Phase 1+ · cf. doc 02 SF reciprocidad)

**Tier 2 · Warm intros**
- Mutual connection introduces founder to target operator
- Inherited trust via connector credibility
- LinkedIn network leverage (doc 02 audience building feeds este)

**Tier 3 · In-person events/visits**
- CANIRAC TJ events · industry gatherings · direct restaurant visits
- Founder physical presence · MX SMB cultural fit
- Trust building face-to-face

**Tier 4 · LinkedIn DM warm**
- Inbound signal (profile view · comment · content engagement from doc 02) → DM follow-up
- Value-first · NO cold pitch · acknowledgment of signal
- Handoff from distribution (doc 02) inbound signals

**Tier 5 · Cold outreach (selective)**
- ONLY when network exhausted + hyper-targeted ICP fit
- NEVER spam · NEVER mass campaigns
- Minimal Phase 0 · selective Phase 1

### 3.3 WhatsApp clarification

**WhatsApp es post-firma CS interface · NOT pre-firma cold outreach channel** (cf. doc 00 §2.5 · `03-competitive-analysis/07-defensibility.md` v0.1 §5.4 uso #3 canonical).

| WhatsApp use | Status |
|---|---|
| Cold outreach prospecting | ❌ NEVER · commoditiza moat |
| Warm follow-up post-network-intro (if operator shares WhatsApp) | ⚠️ Acceptable IF warm + operator-initiated sharing |
| Post-firma agency operational interface | ✅ Canonical uso #3 |

### 3.4 Channel evolution per phase

| Phase | Outreach channel mix |
|---|---|
| Phase 0 (Q3 2026-Q1 2027) | Network referrals heavy · warm intros · in-person · CANIRAC |
| Phase 1 (Q1-Q2 2027) | Network + warm intros + LinkedIn DM warm (inbound maturing) + selective cold |
| Phase 1.5+ (Q3 2027+) | Above + channel partners ramp (La Canasta · consultor partners cf. doc 05) + selective paid outreach automation (Path B) |

## 4. Multi-stakeholder outreach sequencing

### 4.1 Frame · multi-threading 68% pre-cierre

> Multi-stakeholder engagement (Owner + Manager + Chef + Contable) es 3x conversion lever · single-thread = structural underperformance NOT optimization preference (cf. doc 00 §2.3 · `02-customer-research/05-buying-process.md` v0.7 §10).

**Outreach implication:** outreach planea multi-stakeholder mapping desde primer contacto · NOT solo Owner.

### 4.2 Entry point sequencing canonical

| Stakeholder | Entry timing | Outreach role |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Entry point primary (paga · approval autoridad final) | First contact · sets discovery interview |
| **Manager** | Post-Owner interest (Sesión 1 demo · cf. doc 04) | Operational champion development |
| **Chef** | Post-Manager (Sesión 2 demo) | Adoption stickiness signal |
| **Contable externo** | Parallel track from Stage C | Silent veto mitigation early (cf. `02-customer-research/05-buying-process.md` v0.7 §10.8) |

### 4.3 Owner-first rationale

`[Hipótesis sin validar]` · Owner-first porque:
- Owner paga + approval autoridad final
- Owner sets tone para multi-stakeholder engagement
- Owner introduces Manager/Chef/Contable (internal credibility)

**Validation trigger:** behavioral first 3 design partners · ¿Owner-first óptimo vs Manager-first en algún caso?

### 4.4 Multi-stakeholder mapping pre-discovery

Durante outreach (pre-discovery), founder identifica buying committee:

| Question | Purpose |
|---|---|
| "¿Quién más decide en tu operación?" | Identify Owner + Manager + Chef + Contable structure |
| "¿Tienes gerente operativo?" | Manager identification |
| "¿Quién maneja tu contabilidad/fiscal?" | Contable identification (silent veto early) |
| "¿Tu chef participa en decisiones operativas?" | Chef adoption signal |

### 4.5 Anti-pattern single-thread

> Outreach solo a Owner sin plan multi-stakeholder = 3x lower conversion structural. Single-thread NO es optimization preference · es structural underperformance documentado.

## 5. Outreach messaging + hooks

### 5.1 Messaging principles

Anclado VP statement §6 + voice/tone 9 principles:

| Principio | Application outreach |
|---|---|
| Lenguaje del operador | NO tech jargon · operator vernacular (cf. verbatim library) |
| Sin hype | NO "revolucionario" · NO "game-changer" · honest framing |
| Concreto sobre abstracto | Specific pain reference · NO generic value props |
| El por qué (no solo el qué) | Reasoning + context · NO feature-list |
| Honestidad sobre marketing | Discovery ask NOT sale ask · transparent intent |

### 5.2 Hook frameworks per channel

| Channel | Hook approach |
|---|---|
| Network referral | Mutual connection context + specific pain relevance |
| Warm intro | Shared connection credibility + curiosity (NOT pitch) |
| In-person | Industry presence + peer recognition + casual |
| LinkedIn DM warm | Inbound signal acknowledgment + value-first |
| Cold (selective) | Hyper-specific pain + zero generic + research-evident |

### 5.3 Message templates canonical

Templates canonical viven en `01-propuesta-de-valor/03-vp-statement-compressed.md` v0.1 §6 (NO re-redraw · cite):
- WhatsApp cold outreach template (warm-context only)
- Email per stakeholder template (Owner · Manager · Chef · Contable)

**Disciplina:** customize per prospect · NO copy-paste generic · operator language fluency demonstrated.

### 5.4 Messaging by stakeholder

| Stakeholder | Angle primary |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | ROI + tranquilidad operativa + escalamiento ordenado (financial decision lens) |
| **Manager** | Operational value + workflow simplification + tiempo recuperado (daily-use lens) |
| **Chef** | Recetario standardization + augmentar criterio (NO reemplazar) (adoption lens) |
| **Contable externo** | CFDI compliance + data limpia defensa SAT + LFPDPPP comfort (compliance lens) |

### 5.5 Discovery ask framing

> The "yes" we want = **discovery interview** · NOT demo · NOT sale. Low-commitment ask.

| Ask framing | Example |
|---|---|
| ✅ Low-commitment discovery | "¿Te late platicar 30 min sobre cómo manejas tu operación? Sin compromiso · solo quiero entender tu día a día" |
| ❌ High-commitment premature | "¿Quieres ver una demo de Zenet?" (premature · pre-discovery) |

### 5.6 Pre-discovery prep ask (operational)

Post-discovery-booking, pre-Sesión 1, operational ask que converts demo a his-numbers-displayed (cf. `02-customer-research/04-customer-journey-detallado.md` v0.7 §3.6.7):

> "Antes de vernos · mándame foto de 5 facturas recientes + lista de tus 10 recetas más vendidas. Así te muestro algo con TUS números · no genérico."

**Razón:** converts generic show-and-tell a his-numbers-displayed · dramatically higher engagement Sesión 1.

## 6. Outreach funnel + conversion expectations

### 6.1 Outreach funnel canonical

```
Targeted touches (outreach attempts)
  ↓ response rate
Responses (engaged)
  ↓ discovery booking rate
Discovery interviews booked
  ↓ qualified rate (post-discovery · cf. doc 02 SF §3 criterios)
Qualified prospects → 10-step lifecycle (doc 02 SF §8)
```

### 6.2 Conversion expectations per channel

`[Hipótesis sin validar]` · refine post-pilot first 20 outreach attempts:

| Channel | Response rate | Discovery booking rate | Combined (touch → discovery) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Network referrals | 60-80% | 50-70% | 30-50% |
| Warm intros | 50-70% | 45-65% | 25-45% |
| In-person | 70-90% (face-to-face) | 30-50% | 20-35% |
| LinkedIn DM warm | 30-50% | 30-50% | 15-25% |

## 7. Channel-specific playbooks

### 7.1 Network referral playbook

**Activation mechanics:**
- Map founder's existing network: operadores TJ/BC known · industry contacts · prior relationships
- Direct ask: relationship-leveraged · honest intent
- Socios Fundadores referrals (Phase 1+): 1-3 warm intros/año reciprocidad (cf. doc 02 SF)

**Ask example:**
> "Oye [nombre] · estoy construyendo Zenet para operadores como tú. ¿Te late platicar 30 min sobre cómo manejas tu operación? Sin venta · solo quiero aprender de tu experiencia."

### 7.2 Warm intro playbook

**Mechanics:**
- Identify mutual connections (LinkedIn network · industry · founder's network)
- Intro request framing: específico + low-burden para connector
- Follow-up post-intro: acknowledge connector + value-first

**Intro request example (to connector):**
> "[Connector] · ¿conoces a [target operator]? Estoy construyendo algo para operadores 2-3 sucursales · me encantaría platicar con él/ella. ¿Me harías el intro si crees que tiene sentido?"

### 7.3 In-person playbook

**Mechanics:**
- CANIRAC events · industry gatherings · direct restaurant visits (TJ geographic)
- Founder physical presence · casual relationship building (NOT hard pitch)
- In-person evaluation imperativo GTM #7

**Direct visit approach:**
- Visit during off-peak hours (mid-afternoon · NOT rush)
- Casual conversation · industry curiosity · NO immediate pitch
- Build relationship → discovery ask natural

### 7.4 CANIRAC TJ playbook

**CANIRAC TJ touchpoint** (cf. `01-industry-and-market/06-estructura-y-ecosistema.md` v1.0.1 §7.3):
- Liderazgo Rebeca Aguilar Santuario
- Bootcamp GastronomIA Enero 2026 (40 marcas / 203 restaurantes participantes)

**Engagement mechanics hipótesis:**

| Approach | Status hipótesis |
|---|---|
| Networking (attend · connect) | ⭐ Primary Phase 0 |
| Speaking opportunity | Phase 1+ (post case studies + credibility) |
| Sponsorship | Defer (cost · NO Phase 0 budget) |

**Decisión abierta:** CANIRAC mechanics exact · trigger Bootcamp Enero 2026 RSVP.

### 7.5 LinkedIn DM warm playbook

**Handoff from distribution (doc 02) inbound signals:**
- Inbound signal: profile view · comment · content engagement from ICP profile
- DM follow-up: acknowledge signal + value-first + low-commitment discovery ask
- NO cold spam · NO generic templated

**DM example (warm · post-engagement):**
> "Hola [nombre] · vi que te interesó mi post sobre [tema]. Estoy construyendo Zenet para operadores como tú · ¿te late platicar 30 min sobre cómo manejas tu operación?"

### 7.6 Cold outreach playbook (selective)

**ONLY when network exhausted + hyper-targeted ICP fit:**
- Research-evident (NOT spray): specific operator · specific pain reference
- Hyper-specific hook · zero generic
- Disciplina anti-spam: max 2-3 follow-ups · respect signal

**Cold disciplina:**
- ❌ NO mass campaigns
- ❌ NO lead list buying
- ✅ Selective · researched · hyper-targeted only
