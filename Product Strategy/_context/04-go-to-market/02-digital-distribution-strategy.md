|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Very High | High | High (text + image) | Native 2024-2026 | Very High | **🥇 Best fit** |

### 2.3 Por qué LinkedIn gana para Zenet específicamente · 6 razones

| # | Razón | Detalle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Audience density target ICP** | Operadores 2-3 sucursales serios (Sub-segmento B beachhead) · contadores externos · founders LATAM B2B SaaS · pre-seed/seed investors (Latitud · ALLVP · Cometa) · advisors B2B SaaS · future hires sales/CS/eng MX · TODOS genuinely activos LinkedIn |

| 4 | **Founder bandwidth efficiency más alta** | Text + image production vs video production · LinkedIn-native bandwidth efficient para founder solo bootstrap |
| 5 | **Algorithm rewards consistency + engagement quality** | Committed creators get exponential reach · sustainable cadence + engagement quality > posting frequency raw |
| 6 | **Investor/advisor warm intros density very high LATAM** | Latitud Ventures · ALLVP · 500 Startups LATAM · Cometa Capital founders + partners LinkedIn-active · pre-Series A prep value compounds |

### 5.4 Anti-formats Phase 1

- ❌ **NO video Phase 1** (production effort ceiling · founder bandwidth)
- ❌ **NO native LinkedIn polls** (low engagement quality Phase 1 · vanity engagement)
- ❌ **NO LinkedIn newsletters Phase 1** (defer Phase 1.5+ consideration)
- ❌ **NO sales-heavy posts** (destroys agency framing · cliente-perceived spam)

### 5.5 Cadence framework hipótesis · 4 posts/sem core + flex

| Día tipo | Activity | Pillar primary |
|---|---|---|
| Lunes | Industry insight + commentary | 2 |
| Martes | Founder journey building in public update | 1 |
| Miércoles | OFF (engagement-only · comments + DMs) | — |
| Jueves | **Technical deep-dive** | **3** |
| Viernes | Operator vernacular reflection OR Behind-scenes (alternating weeks) | 4 / 5 |
| Sábado-Domingo | OFF (rest + engagement light) | — |
| **Flex 5th post optional** | Case study OR long-form synthesis | 1 / 6 |

**Total: 4 posts/sem core · adjustable 3-5 según bandwidth founder semanal.**

**Disciplina anti-burnout:** if founder energy + content ready → add 5th post · if bandwidth saturated → hold at 4 · decision per week NOT rigid commitment.

**Validation trigger:** pilot 4 sem · LinkedIn analytics + engagement signals · refinement post pillars allocation + cadence + optimal times.
