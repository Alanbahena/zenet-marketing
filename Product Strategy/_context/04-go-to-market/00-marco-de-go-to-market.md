## 2. Frames estratégicos GTM

Siete frames invariables que gobiernan toda decisión GTM downstream. Cualquier decisión que contradiga uno requiere relitigar el frame primero — no rebasarlo silenciosamente.

### 2.1 Founder-led sales Phase 1 · NO premature sales hire

**Frame:** founder visibility es load-bearing pre-PMF Phase 1 · trust building requires founder presence en discovery + cierre.

**Anclaje:** `02-customer-research/03-pains-y-workarounds.md` v0.5 §5.10 imperativo GTM #3 ("founder visibility").

**Implicación operativa:**
- Founder Alan ejecuta sales motion completo Phase 0 (cohort 5 design partners)
- Phase 1.5+ trigger evaluado: primer CS hire MX rate ($10-15K MXN/mes junior)
- Anti-pattern: hiring sales/CS antes de design partner cohort validated

### 2.2 In-person primary sales motion · MX SMB cultural fit

**Frame:** sales motion requires in-person primary touchpoints · video-only insuficiente MX SMB cultural pattern.

**Anclaje:** `02-customer-research/03-pains.md` §5.10 imperativo #7 ("in-person evaluation").

**Implicación operativa:**
- Phase 1 design partners require in-person mínimo 1-2 sessions (Sesión 1 Manager + Sesión 3 Owner cierre)
- Geographic Phase 1 Tijuana primary · alinea con founder physical presence
- LATAM Phase 5 expansion adjusts model · NOT Phase 1 concern

### 2.3 Multi-threading 68% load-bearing · NO single-thread accepted

**Frame:** multi-stakeholder engagement (Owner + Manager + Chef + Contable) es 3x conversion lever · single-thread = structural underperformance NOT optimization preference.

**Anclaje:** `02-customer-research/05-buying-process.md` v0.7 §10.

**Implicación operativa:**
- Three-session demo structure canonical (cf. §3.2 framework #1) ENFORCE multi-threading
- Single-thread con dueño solo = expected lower conversion
- Sales motion always plans multi-stakeholder routing pre-cierre

### 2.4 Peer reference > paid acquisition · MX cultural primary

**Frame:** peer reference (operador conocido TJ/BC recomienda Zenet) supera paid acquisition (LinkedIn ads · Google ads) en MX SMB cultural pattern.

**Anclaje:** `02-customer-research/06-objeciones-y-fricciones-de-adopcion.md` v0.5 §4.9 MX-specific retention lever.

**Implicación operativa:**
- Phase 1: cero paid acquisition · disciplina canonical
- Socios Fundadores reciprocidad incluye 1-3 warm intros/año peer operators
- Phase 1.5+ reactivation trigger evaluated · NOT default
- LinkedIn organic ONLY Phase 1 (cf. doc 02 digital distribution)

### 2.5 WhatsApp Business uso #3 post-firma CS · NOT pre-firma sales channel

**Frame:** WhatsApp Business es post-firma operational interface (uso #3 canonical) · NOT pre-firma cold outreach channel.

**Anclaje:** `03-competitive-analysis/07-diferenciacion-zenet-y-defensibility.md` v0.1 §5.4.

**Implicación operativa:**
- Pre-firma sales = LinkedIn DM warm intros + in-person + network referrals
- Post-firma CS = WhatsApp agency interface canonical
- Anti-pattern: WhatsApp cold sales outreach commoditiza moat · misuse

### 2.6 95% adoption/coexistence vs 5% switching framing

**Frame:** Sales motion canonical narrative = *"trabajamos sobre tu POS · NO lo reemplazamos"* · 95% Zenet scenarios MX son additive coexistence vs replacement.

**Anclaje:** `03-competitive-analysis/06-switching-dynamics-y-incumbente.md` v0.1.

**Implicación operativa:**
- Discovery question primary: *"¿qué sistema usas hoy?"*
- 4 scenarios A/B/C/D identification customize approach
- NO compete frontalmente con POS+inventario · agency layer encima

### 2.7 GTM bidireccional con capital subfolder

**Frame:** GTM operates en bidireccional relationship con `05-capital-y-finanzas/`:

| Direction | How |
|---|---|
| Capital → GTM (constraint) | Path A solo bootstrap vs Path B pre-seed bridge SHAPE GTM execution capability |
| GTM → Capital (informs) | GTM operational needs INFORM funding sizing requirements |

**Implicación operativa:**
- Doc 01 GTM playbook §3 modela Path A vs B scenarios explícitamente
- GTM doesn't OWN funding decision · informs operationally
- Boundary preserved · capital subfolder owns Path A/B decision finalmente

### 2.8 Platform vision context · light-touch frame (informa Phase 1.5+ partnership · NO Phase 1 decision)

**Frame:** Long-term Zenet vision includes platform play · multi-supplier procurement marketplace con Zenet como cognitive nucleus. Esto informa Phase 1.5+ partnership negotiation (specifically La Canasta) sin distract Phase 1 execution.

**Anclaje:**
- `Market Research/05-market-insights/01-vision-plataforma-zenet.md` v0.1 (platform play exploration)
- `02-features-y-scope/01-roadmap-hipotesis-fase-1.5-y-2.md` v0.1 F8 partner channel strategy

**Two-phase La Canasta relationship hypothesis:**

| Phase | La Canasta relationship |
|---|---|
| Phase 1.5 (Q1-Q2 2027) | Channel referral partner · introduces Zenet a sus restaurant customers · referral fees + co-marketing |
| Phase 2.5/3 (Q1 2028+) | **Pilot supplier en Zenet procurement platform marketplace** · early-mover advantage · transaction flow access |

**Implicación operativa:**
- Phase 1: NO mention platform vision en design partner conversations (focus product-market fit)
- Phase 1.5: La Canasta partnership negotiation INCLUDES platform vision context (mutual long-term strategic positioning vs single-purpose referral)
- Phase 2+: Platform features ship · partnership evolves naturally

**Anti-pattern:** front-load platform vision Phase 1 (distract resources · confuse positioning Phase 1 agency-as-SaaS focus).

## 6. Disciplina + principios estructurales

### 6.1 Hipótesis labeling explícito

Tres tags canónicos en uso cross-doc:
- `[Hipótesis sin validar]` — número/decisión sin evidencia conductual
- `[Estimación cualitativa]` — derivado de research backbone o triangulación
- `[Anclado en research X]` — directamente soportado por doc fuente con cita inline

### 6.2 Frameworks como REFERENCE docs · NO daily checklists

> Framework stack canonical (14 frameworks total · 9 primary + 5 secondary) **NO se aplican simultáneamente en daily execution**. Founder/sales rep aplica 3-4 frameworks máximo per decisión. **Framework theatre** (aplicar 13+ frameworks per execution moment) es anti-pattern explícito.

**Daily execution prioritization:**

| Situation | Frameworks core |
|---|---|
| Discovery call | Three-session + 7 imperativos + LAER |
| Objection moment | LAER + Multi-stakeholder validation |
| Pipeline review | Sales cycle 8 stages + 3-lane timing + ICP scoring |
| CS check-in | Multi-stakeholder + Customer Health Score |
| LinkedIn content | Building in public + 7 imperativos founder visibility |

### 6.3 GTM bidireccional con capital · clarification critical

- GTM consume Path A vs B como INPUT (NOT decision)
- GTM operational needs INFORM funding sizing requirements
- Boundary preserved · ningún subfolder owns ambos decisiones

### 6.4 Cross-references extensive · NO duplicación

GTM subfolder es el más cross-referenced del Product Strategy department:
- ~20 docs upstream heavily referenced
- Disciplina: cite specific sections + version · NO redibuja info upstream
- Anti-pattern: copy-paste content desde source docs · destruye single source of truth

### 6.5 v0.2 refinement trigger explícito post Mes 3 data

> Framework stack y decisiones canónicas son **BEST AVAILABLE EVIDENCE pre-PMF** · NOT validated con behavioral data. **v0.2 trigger explícito:** 3+ design partners Mes 3 GO/NO-GO completed + behavioral data + framework adjustments validated/invalidated. Frameworks pueden necesitar refinement substantive post Mes 3 design partner data.

**Specifically watching for v0.2 adjustments:**
- Three-session demo timing real vs hipótesis (2-4 vs 4-8 weeks)
- LAER techniques actual effectiveness MX-context
- Multi-stakeholder cadence real vs hipótesis
- Customer Health Score formula calibration
- Sales-led vs hybrid model first signals
